# Proofreading notes: Divine Liturgy, Robertson (1894)

Doubtful readings found while checking the transcription against the page scans (25 Oct 2026).
Robertson's misprints are kept as printed; where only an accent, breathing or apostrophe failed to print, it was filled in.

## Pages 232–245

- p.238: in 'τὸ Ὄνομα τὸ ἅγιον αὐτοῦ' (Ps. 102:1) the first letter of αὐτοῦ looks like ο in the print ('οὐτοῦ'), probably a damaged α; transcribed αὐτοῦ.
- p.238/240/244: smooth vs rough breathing on αὐτοῦ in the Psalm verses is hard to tell in this typeface; every instance looked smooth to me and is transcribed αὐτοῦ (the OCR read αὑτοῦ in several places).
- p.236: 'τῷ Ἁγίῳ, Πνεύματι' has a comma-like mark after Ἁγίῳ in the print; kept as printed (possibly a stray mark).
- p.240: 'τοὺς φοβουβένους αὐτόν' is printed with β (misprint for φοβουμένους); kept as printed.
- p.240: 'πάσαι αἱ Δυνάμεις' is printed with an acute (not πᾶσαι); kept as printed.
- p.244: 'Τὸν φυλάσσαντα ἀλήθειαν' is printed so (LXX usually φυλάσσοντα); kept as printed.
- p.242: 'Καὶ Ψάλλεται ὁμοίως' appears with a capital Ψ in the print; kept (the parallel rubric on p.236 has lower-case ψάλλεται).
- p.244: the first three lines of the page (end of the rubric 'Ἀντίφωνον, ἢ τὰ Τυπικά ... Εὐχῇ' and the heading 'Εὐχὴ Ἀντιφώνου δευτέρου') are faint; accents and breathings on 'Ἀντίφωνον', 'ἢ τὰ', 'Ἁγίου ἑορταζομένου', 'Εὐχὴν τοῦ Ἀντιφώνου· ὁ δὲ' and 'Εὐχὴ' are partly lost and were read from the visible traces, matching the parallel rubric on p.236.
- Numeral sign in 'Ἦχος πλ. δʹ', 'Ψαλ. ρβʹ' and 'Ψαλ. ρμεʹ' written as ʹ (U+02B9, the NFC form of the Greek numeral sign); on p.244 it is printed like an acute on ε ('ρμέ').
- p.236/240/242: initial 'Ὅτι' printed with a worn breathing+accent combination; transcribed Ὅτι.
- p.232: the heading is in unaccented capitals in the print and is transcribed so; the three title lines are combined into one heading item.
- p.234: a small dot after 'οἰκούντων' (before 'ἐν αὐταῖς') looks like a scan mark and was left out; the English has no comma there.
- p.242/243: Greek prints 'Ἀμήν.' on its own line after 'Καὶ νῦν ... αἰώνων.'; English runs 'ages. Amen.' together; combined into one item.

## Pages 246–259

- p.246/247: first item continues Psalm 145 from p.244/245. English p.247 opens with the tail of the previous clause, 'wise the blind:' (the Greek for it, Κύριος σοφοῖ τυφλούς, is on p.244), so the English of item 1 has three extra words that its Greek lacks.
- p.246: κατεῤῥαγμένους: the marks over the double rho are faint; read as ῤῥ.
- p.246: Υἱὸς (grave) and εἷς ὢν: small accents, read as printed best I can see.
- p.246: 'Ἔτι, καὶ ἔτι' printed with an acute only and no visible breathing on the capital (broken sort); transcribed Ἔτι.
- p.246: the second 'Ὁ Χορός·' is printed with no breathing on the Ο (broken sort); transcribed Ὁ.
- p.248: second 'ἐν τῇ Βασιλείᾳ σου' is printed 'τῃ' with no circumflex visible (dropped mark); transcribed τῇ.
- p.250: rubric prints 'Μακάριοι οἱ ἐλεήμνονες' (sic, extra ν), kept as printed; the verse itself above reads ἐλεήμονες.
- p.250: 'Στ΄' (six) printed as Σ+τ, not the stigma ligature; kept as Στ΄.
- p.250: 'Δόξα . . . .' and 'Καὶ νῦν . . . .' printed as spaced dots; written '....'. Same on p.254. English 'Glory . . .' written '...'.
- p.252: 'καὶ ἀεὶ εἰς καὶ τοὺς αἰῶνας' — Robertson's word order (misprint for 'καὶ ἀεί, καὶ εἰς'), kept as printed.
- p.253: stray marks in the English ('ages.’ Amen', 'Hegumen,˰ if', 'who. kisseth') treated as scan specks and left out.
- p.254: in the Troparia rubric 'δηλόνοτι' is printed with the accent on the second syllable (sic), whereas the Σῶσον ἡμᾶς line has δηλονότι; both kept as printed.
- p.255: last word printed 'hee.' with the 't' missing/not inked; transcribed 'thee'. 'Make haste ta' read as 'to' (damaged o). 'Mother of God; but' — the punctuation looks like a semicolon.
- p.256: rubric prints 'λέγαι' (sic, for λέγει), kept as printed.
- p.256: in the Trisagion lines several Ἅγιος have broken breathings (third Ἅγιος of the first line looks like acute only; second Ἅγιος of the last line has no mark at all); all transcribed Ἅγιος.
- p.256: 'Καὶ νῦν ... τῶν αἰώνων Ἀμήν.' has no full stop after αἰώνων in print; kept so. The inline 'Ἐκ γ΄.' / 'Thrice.' at the end of the first Trisagion line made a separate rubric item.
- p.258: 'κατ εἰκόνα' printed with a gap but no visible apostrophe; transcribed κατ’ εἰκόνα.
- p.256–259: the Trisagion prayer runs across the page turn (256→258 Greek, 257→259 English); kept as one item.

## Pages 260–273

- p.260: Δαυῒδ (twice) — the accent on the diaeresis iota is too small to tell grave from acute; written Δαυῒδ.
- p.264: εἰπῶν is printed with a circumflex (expected εἰπὼν); kept as printed.
- p.264: αἶρει appears printed with a circumflex (expected αἴρει); kept as printed, but in this typeface smooth breathing + acute on iota is hard to tell from a circumflex.
- p.266/268: Εἴπωμεν, εἴπωμεν, Οἴκου — the iota mark looks circumflex-like as in αἶρει above; written with smooth breathing + acute (ἴ).
- p.268: first choir line is printed 'Ο Χορός. Κύριε ἐλέησον. Εκ γʹ.' with no breathings on Ο and Εκ and a full stop instead of the raised dot (also the last choir line on p.268 lacks the breathing on Ο); normalised to the usual 'Ὁ Χορός· … Ἐκ γʹ.' (ink failure).
- p.270: 'ὁψηλοῖς' — printed with an o-like letter (round, not υ-shaped), apparently a misprint of ὑψηλοῖς; kept as an omicron with rough breathing. Breathing (rough vs smooth) not clearly legible.
- p.270: 'Ἵνα καὶ αὐτοὶ' and 'Ἐκφώνησις.' — breathings not visible in the scan (only an accent before Ι; a blot on Ε); written Ἵνα, Ἐκφώνησις.
- p.270: 'Ἀποστολικῇ' in the Catechumens prayer has a grave-like mark instead of a smooth breathing; written Ἀ.
- p.272: 'Ἔτι, καὶ ἔτι' — only an acute is visible before Ε, no breathing; written Ἔτι.
- p.272: 'τοῦ Ἁγίου Πνεύματος,' — the mark after Πνεύματος may be a comma or semicolon (smudged); written as comma.
- p.270: 'τοῦ Πατρὸς,' printed with a grave before the comma; kept.

## Pages 274–287

- p.274: 'Ὅτι' at start of the Exclamation: the breathing mark is faint/damaged (looks like a bare acute); transcribed Ὅτι.
- p.274: printed 'εἰς τοῦς αἰῶνας' (circumflex on τοῦς) — kept as printed.
- p.274: printed 'πνεύματας' (for πνεύματος) — kept as printed.
- p.274: printed 'συνσεως' with no accent (for συνέσεως) — kept as printed.
- p.276: 'Υμνος' in the rubric before the Cherubic Hymn is printed with no visible breathing/accent — kept as printed.
- p.276: 'Αλλ’ ὅμως' printed without breathing on Αλλ’ — kept as printed.
- p.278: printed 'φιλανθρπωπίαν' (misprint for φιλανθρωπίαν) — kept as printed.
- p.280: printed 'τὰ χεῖρας' (for τὰς χεῖρας) — kept as printed.
- p.280/282/284/286: 'αὐτοῦ' after Βασιλείᾳ is printed with a smooth breathing (not αὑτοῦ) as far as the scan shows.
- p.282: printed 'ἔνδον των ἁγίων' (των unaccented) and 'τῶν αἰῶνων' (circumflex) in the Priest's reply — kept as printed.
- p.282: 'μετᾶ Λῃστοῦ' — the accent on μετα looks like a circumflex (probably a damaged grave); transcribed as printed, μετᾶ.
- p.282: 'ἐν ᾍδου' — breathing/accent mark on Ἅδου partly damaged; read as rough + acute with iota subscript.
- p.284: the mark after 'τὴν κεφαλήν' in the rubric is a raised comma-like mark; transcribed as a comma.
- p.286: the Priest's 'Μνησθείη σου Κύριος...' ends 'τῶν αἰώνων.' with no Ἀμήν, but the English p.287 ends 'Amen.' (the Greek then has the Deacon say Ἀμήν).
- p.286: first 'Ο Χορός·' printed without breathing — kept as printed in the label.
- p.286: printed 'ὀργῆς κινδύνου' without a comma between them — kept as printed.

## Pages 288–301

- p.288: 'τῆς χαριτός σου' printed with the accent on -τός only (as printed, not χάριτός).
- p.290: speaker label printed 'Ὁ Διάκονος.' with a full stop, not a raised dot; kept as printed.
- p.293: no comma after 'the dead' in Creed VII ('the living and the dead of whose Kingdom'); kept as printed.
- p.292/294: Creed article numerals printed as Α', Β' ... ΙΒ'; transcribed with the Greek numeral sign (U+0374, which NFC turns into U+02B9 ʹ). Article Η' (p.294) has no full stop after it, unlike the others; kept as printed.
- p.294: 'Καὶ Ζωὴν' (Creed XII) — in this Greek typeface lowercase ζ looks like capital Ζ, so capital vs lowercase cannot be told apart; capitalised to match the English 'Life'. Elsewhere ζωή is given lowercase.
- p.296: 'ὡσαύτος ὤν' printed with omicron (ὡσαύτος, not ὡσαύτως); kept as printed.
- p.296: no comma printed between 'σὲ αἰνεῖν' and 'σοὶ εὐχαριστεῖν'; kept as printed.
- p.296: stray mark after 'κεκραγότα,' omitted as a scan mark.
- p.300: 'Κατάπαμψον' printed (misprint for Κατάπεμψον); kept as printed.
- p.292 vs p.300: Orarion printed 'Ὡράριον' (rough breathing) on p.292 but 'Ὠραρίῳ' (smooth breathing) on p.300; each kept as printed.
- p.301: punctuation after 'and for all' is a faint mark that could be a comma or full stop; transcribed as a comma.

## Pages 302–315

- p.304/305: the Greek rubric ends 'εἰ δὲ μή, τὸ ἑπόμενον εἰς Ἦχον β΄.' but the English has only 'but if not, the following:' (no 'in Tone 2'); kept as printed on each side.
- p.304: no full stop printed between 'τῶν Κεκοιμημένων' and 'Μνημονεύει δὲ καθ’ ἑαυτὸν'; transcribed without one. Breathing on 'ἑαυτὸν' and the mark on 'Ἦχον' are faint; read as ἑ- and Ἦ-.
- p.304: 'Θεομητορικὴ' accent faint (grave assumed; could be acute).
- p.308: Greek prints 'Ἐπισκόπο' (final -υ missing at line end); transcribed as printed.
- p.310: 'Δώρων' and 'ἐξελθών' are poorly printed (ω looks like 'ιύ'); read as ω. 'συγχῶρησιν' is printed with a circumflex; kept as printed.
- p.311: English prints 'standiug' (misprint for 'standing'); kept as printed.
- p.312: the third choir response 'Παράσχου Κύριε' has no full stop in print; kept as printed.
- p.302 has 'κρῖμα' (circumflex) while p.310 has 'κρίμα' (acute); both as printed.

## Pages 316–329

- p.318/319: the IC NI KA XC seal diagram is given as one rubric item; Greek side uses Greek letters with lunate sigma (ΙϹ ΝΙ ΚΑ ΧϹ), English side Latin letters as printed; line breaks of the diagram shown with ' / '.
- p.320: 'τὸ ὄνομα ΙϹ.,' — the full stop after IC is very faint in the scan (English has 'IC.,').
- p.320: 'ἡ ζέσις' (lowercase) vs. 'Ζέσις πίστεως' (capital, line start): in this bold font lowercase ζ resembles Z, so the case of ζέσις is a judgement call.
- p.322: printed 'Εἶτα.' with a full stop (English has 'Then,'); kept as printed.
- p.326: 'εἰς ἄφεσιν αὐτοῦ ἁμαρτιῶν' — breathing on αὐτοῦ appears smooth in the scan; kept as printed.
- p.328: 'τὸ ἐπόμενον' printed with smooth breathing (p.318 has 'ἑπόμενον'); kept as printed. Also 'ὁ ἐρχομένος' accented on the penult as printed.
- p.324: 'Καί λαμβάνει' printed with acute on Καί; kept as printed.
