# Privacy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Orthodox is a free, non-profit app. This page says plainly what happens to information about you.

## What the app does not do

It has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not sell or share information about you. The people who make the app receive nothing about you from it.

## What stays on your phone

Your settings, parish, bookmarks, notes, reminders and downloaded texts are saved only on your phone. Questions typed into Ask about Orthodoxy are searched on your phone and are not sent anywhere or saved. If you make a backup of your notes, the file goes where you choose to put it.

## Your location

If you ask for churches near you, the phone tells the app where you are, and the app uses this only to sort the list. It is not saved or sent. If you ask for directions, your maps app opens with the church's address.

## The microphone

If you follow a service by listening, the phone listens only while you do so, to find your place in the text. The app saves no sound or words. Where the phone can recognise speech by itself it does so. Where it cannot, the sound may go to Apple's or Google's speech service, under their own privacy terms.

## When the app uses the internet

Some things need the internet: downloading texts (from GitHub), a parish's news, calendar and videos (from its website, Google Calendar and YouTube), and any link you open. As with any website, those services can see your phone's internet address when it connects, and each has its own privacy terms. YouTube videos play through YouTube's privacy-enhanced player.

## Children

The app does not ask anyone's age or collect information from children.

## Removing your information

Deleting the app removes what it has saved on your phone, except any backup you made elsewhere.

## Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first, with its new date.
